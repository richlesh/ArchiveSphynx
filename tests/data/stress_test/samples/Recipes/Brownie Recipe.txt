Brownies

1 1/4 Cup Natural Cocoa Powder
1 cup Brown Sugar
1 cup Sugar
1/2 cup Flour
1/2 tsp Salt
8 oz melted Butter
2 tsp Vanilla Extract
4 large Eggs

1. Make emulsion of the eggs by beating in mixer.

2. On slow speed add sifted dry ingredients.

3. Slowly add liquid ingredients.

4. Pour in pan coated with non-stick spray then wax paper.

5. Bake for 45 minutes at 300F
